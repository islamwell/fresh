How the Kids Curriculum Works

📖

Semester I: Foundations

Arabic alphabet recognition, makharij (articulation points), basic Tajweed, and introduction to Tauheed and Allah’s creation.

🤲

Semester II: Practice

Memorization of Surah Al-Fatiha and short Surahs (Juz Amma), daily morning and evening Athkar, and Salah step-by-step.

🌟

Semester III: Character

Stories of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and the Sahaba, respecting parents, honesty, kindness to animals, and Muslim identity.
