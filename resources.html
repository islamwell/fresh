Recommended Islamic Learning Websites

Trusted digital Quranic search engines, authentic Hadith databases, and classical Tafseer libraries for serious students of knowledge.

1. Quran Explorer ↗

Multi-language recitations, synchronous text highlighting, and verse translations.

2. Tanzil.net ↗

Highly verified Quranic Unicode text project, translations in 40+ languages, and audio recitations.

3. SearchTruth ↗

Rapid word search engine across the Quran and Major Hadith collections.

4. Quranic Arabic Corpus ↗

Annotated linguistic resource showing Arabic grammar, syntax, and morphology for every word.

5. Sunnah.com ↗

The definitive searchable repository of the Prophet's ﷺ sayings with Arabic and English side-by-side.

6. Altafsir.com ↗

Immense archive of classical and contemporary Quranic commentaries (Tafseer Ibn Katheer, Tabari, Qurtubi).
