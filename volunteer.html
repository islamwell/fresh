Invest Your Time as a Sadaqah Jariyah

NurulQuran is driven by dedicated brothers and sisters around the globe who generously give their time and talents to spread the light of the Quran. Whether you have 2 hours a week or more, your unique skills can make an eternal difference.

Available Volunteer Opportunities

🎨

Media, Design & Video Editing

Create event flyers, social media carousels, YouTube thumbnails, video reels, and polish audio recordings for our lecture catalog.

Photoshop • Premiere • Canva • Audio
💻

IT & Web Development

Build and optimize our web apps, manage Firebase/hosting services, configure automated audio streaming, and support Zoom class infrastructure.

HTML/CSS • JavaScript • Git • Cloud
✍️

Translation & Transcription

Translate Tafseer summaries, lecture notes, and social posts between Urdu, English, and Arabic. Create video subtitles for international outreach.

Urdu • English • Arabic
💬

Student Coordination & Moderation

Support students in official WhatsApp study circles, answer course registration inquiries, record attendance, and facilitate breakout rooms.

Community Care • WhatsApp • Zoom
📍

Local Tour & Event Operations

Help coordinate on-the-ground logistics during international tours, seminars, book tables, attendee registration, and welcome hospitality.

Local City Teams • Events
🤝

General Administration

Help with bookkeeping assistance, email management, documentation, certificate preparation, and special Ramadan projects.

Organization • Documentation
