Programs Designed for Today’s Youth

Core Course • Teens Ages 13+ Girls

🌱 Faith & Character (Imaan & Akhlaq)

An uplifting course exploring the pillars of Iman, overcoming doubts, dealing with peer pressure, building self-worth through Allah’s love, and developing emotional resilience.

🎯 Key Topics:
  • Knowing Allah: Tawheed and His Beautiful Names
  • Stories of Youth in the Quran (Ashab al-Kahf, Maryam)
  • Friendships, Social Media & Healthy Boundaries
  • Finding Peace & Clarity through Salah and Dua

Ramadan Special Girls Only

🌙 Ramadan Program for Teens

A tailored Ramadan journey guiding young women through Quran reflections, purposeful goal setting, understanding the spirit of fasting, and making the most of Laylatul Qadr.

✨ Highlights:
  • Daily Surah & Ayah reflections relevant to youth
  • Personal Ramadan habit tracker & journaling
  • Interactive Q&A on fasting rules and youth questions
  • Live Zoom discussions with qualified mentors
